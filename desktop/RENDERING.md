# Desktop observation model

The desktop isolates the original deterministic simulator in a Node worker. A fixed physical minute remains one call to `step`; rendering, quality, FPS, audio, camera, settings and inspection cannot call it or feed values back. A serialized checkpoint and thoughts cross the process boundary. The renderer loads a separate world copy. All existing code in `v2/src/{core,world,sim,mind,build}` and `v2/src/config.js` is unchanged from release `85e2027`. The source fingerprint is `c494638a78a4b3d5464f9f9d04b56997f659e7a781043bceabc0edaf408be2b2`.

## Ocean geometry

Each original tile is 2 m. East is +X, south +Z and vertical height +Y. The three saved grids still contain finite water, salt, heat, nutrients, marine populations, sediment and bubbles, and the original action/energy ledgers. This release does not substitute a graphics spectrum for those inventories.

For saved action `N_b` (J s), cell wet area `S`, density 1025 kg/m³ and acceleration 9.81 m/s², amplitude is `A_b = sqrt(2 N_b omega_b / (S rho g))`. The original 24 modes have periods 6, 10 and 16 seconds and eight directions each. Their phase at a point is `omega_b (saved_ray_travel - physical_seconds) + saved_phase_x * offset_x + saved_phase_y * offset_z + saved_seed_phase`. The mode height is `A_b sin(phase)`.

The surface adds saved mean elevation to the bilinear interpolation of each cell's complex mode amplitude. Interpolating complex amplitudes avoids false slopes when phase angles wrap or propagation paths differ. The analytic derivatives include both the mode derivative and the interpolation-weight derivative. Dry mean elevations use a wet-weighted quotient and its quotient derivative; completely dry observations return zero height and zero slope and are discarded. The unit normal is `normalize(-height_x, 1, -height_z)`.

The coast is reconstructed at 1 m in High and 0.5 m in Ultra. Parent and child observations are joined over 16 m with the product of four C1 edge weights `s(t) = t² (3 - 2t)`; normals include `(child_height - parent_height) grad(w)`. This remains differentiable where two corner distances are equal. It is an observation of nested fields, not a change to physical cells or their volumes. Rings extend the surface to 20 km beyond the island. Modes above a patch's spatial Nyquist limit are omitted from that patch's drawing; their saved action and all physical consequences remain present. Patch vertices share a world-aligned grid and explicit corners. A fine patch's boundary is constrained to the neighbouring coarse polygon's polyline, preventing rasterization holes at T-junctions. No invented short-wave noise, arbitrary wave amplification or unrelated looping sea animation is added.

CPU phase reduction before Float32 upload keeps the large real-time clock and long saved travel times out of shader subtraction. GPU readback tests compare heights and slopes with the double-precision observer at wet, dry, boundary, parent-grid and 100-year-clock samples. Node tests independently check monochromatic energy, known phase, period, analytic derivatives, dry limits and grid joins.

Floating saved wood and swimming/adrift animals read the same surface. Ship bodies follow it as a graphical equilibrium pose. These poses do not add hull dynamics, buoyant forces or rigid-body motion to the simulator. The original marine drifter transport remains authoritative. Inspection solves the displaced surface along the camera ray. The solver is a reduced finite-volume ocean with a linear action spectrum and depth-limited dissipation, as documented in [OCEAN.md](https://github.com/TheRealGD2X/castaway/blob/main/v2/OCEAN.md); it does not resolve overturning crests, spray droplets or three-dimensional turbulent CFD. The island and shelf use the original flat-domain approximation.

## Light and optics

The sun vector uses the original model's latitude 57° N, longitude 6.5° W, calendar declination and hour angle. Simulated `wx.sun` supplies incident irradiance; 115 lm/W is an explicit luminous-efficacy assumption. Lights share a display normalization of 10,000 lux/candela. Direct light is projected using solar elevation. Diffuse skylight is an empirical cloud-dependent reconstruction, with exponential twilight and a moon contribution. The sky's angular colours are normalized against that same diffuse illuminance, including at dawn. Hemisphere fill, Preetham sky scattering and PMREM environment lighting are approximate visual transport, not a solved spectral atmosphere. Cloud coverage, haze, humidity, rain and visibility read weather; the sky cloud layout has no independent clock-driven motion.

Sun shadows use 4096 samples in High and 8192 in Ultra. Moon position, distance and phase are calculated from the same physical date by Astronomy Engine at the island observer. Moon brightness uses an assumed full-moon reference 0.267 lux, phase-squared scaling, elevation and cloud attenuation. The lunar sphere's lit side uses the solar direction and is also included in the environment map. Moon shadows use 2048 samples. No invented bright night sun is added. Exposure adapts over two seconds and is a camera property, not physical illumination.

Fire point lights use actual combustion power, assumed wood-flame luminous efficacy 0.65 lm/W, ember luminosity and inverse-square attenuation. Their positions are actual hearths and they cast local shadows. The visible flame height and mean tilt are reduced plume projections from heat, gravity and wind. They do not burn fuel or prescribe pulse events. The existing radiation/thermal solver is unchanged.

Water uses dielectric Fresnel reflection, depth-dependent transmission and Beer-Lambert attenuation. The surface refractive index reads saved layer-zero salt and temperature through [Quan and Fry's empirical fit](https://misclab.umeoce.maine.edu/ftp/users/emmanuel/sediment/Tommy/ch_2_ref/QuanandFry1995.pdf), equation 3, at atmospheric pressure and a representative 550 nm wavelength. The graphical fit clamps to its published range, 0–35 g/kg and 0–30 °C; physical inventories are never clamped. This single-wavelength approximation does not render spectral dispersion. Saved suspended sediment and phytoplankton affect an empirical attenuation colour. Saved bubble foam changes transmission and roughness. A single-scattering source term `L_infinity * (1 - exp(-sigma * depth))` reads solar input; it avoids treating a missing screen-space seabed as opaque black water. Bathymetry reads coast relief/bed changes and the saved parent bed, interpolated at the nest join. Bed materials and attenuation coefficients are assumed visual reconstructions.

GTAO supplies contact occlusion; SSR supplies screen-space reflections; a weak bloom completes the image under AgX tone mapping. Screen-space reflection/refraction misses surfaces outside the image and is an approximation to full ray transport. A Float32 scene buffer retains illumination below HalfFloat's precision. Tone mapping and display conversion precede SMAA, so low moonlight is not discarded by antialiasing. Roughness and metalness use single-channel buffers. The scene pass is non-MSAA because WebGPU depth-gather effects require it. Shader layouts and the fog object remain stable as illumination/weather values change. Fog brightness follows incident light. Camera exposure adapts over two seconds across a broad display range; it is an artistic automatic camera, not a calibrated human-vision model.

## Island, bodies and sound

Terrain reads original 2 m elevation, relief, erosion, snow, paths, wetness and salt. Vegetation reads species, size, remaining leaf/tissue mass and autumn state. Buildings draw installed nodes, supports, panels, joints, shells and paid progress; circular support radius follows installed material volume and length. Saved sag, condition, saturation, stock and onsite materials affect their geometry/materials. Downed branches and quarry mass use instancing rather than hiding old island objects.

Human poses reuse original action descriptors, work-contact frequency and saved trails, with two-bone anatomical inverse kinematics. Animals read species, mass, trails and physical behavioural states. Shapes, anatomy, foliage clustering, vessel dimensions and missing vertical coordinates are explicitly assumed graphical reconstructions; they are not new validated biomechanics or botany. Precipitation glyphs statistically represent many real drops; their terminal fall speed is simplified and their flux and wind read the observer's local atmosphere cell. Freshwater height reads saved hydrology: pools contain cubic metres, so cell-average depth is volume / 4 m². Pools use a flat cell surface, not a subcell hydraulic solver. Lake ice thickness uses saved ice volume and an assumed ice density of 917 kg/m³. Freshwater surface optics read salt concentration and temperature.

Audio remains the existing procedural acoustic engine and AudioWorklet. It uses the renderer's observer position and shared physical clock. Rain, wind, vegetation, construction contacts, streams, waves, animal behaviour and burning fuel keep their original causal sources, finite quantities, distance scaling and bounded continuous DSP. It consumes no simulation RNG and uses no recorded repeating ambience.

## Verification and scope

Original-source fingerprint and golden futures were captured before implementation. Tests compare three seed/minute outcomes, a production checkpoint with thoughts and its future, irregular scheduling, a real worker, atomic save recovery and simulation bytes before/after every observer operation. Native QA checks the actual WebGPU backend, long-clock GPU samples, High/Ultra, journal, inspections, sound, camera, weather/time fixtures, native save/export/fullscreen, renderer reload and measured frame rate. Production QA separately checks real UK-minute catch-up, saved state versus original simulation, close persistence and restart.

These checks establish deterministic compatibility and numerical agreement with this simulation. They do not establish complete agreement with the real ocean, measured illumination, physiological outcomes or audio recordings. Rendering cannot make unresolved processes physically accurate. No conservation ledger, ecological coupling, construction rule, scheduled thought API or timestep has been removed or weakened.
