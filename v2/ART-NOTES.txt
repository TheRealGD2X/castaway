REVIEW
art-review.html offers Camp, Rocky clearing, Shoreline and three lighting
studies. It loads an unchanged saved Day 7 island; the clock is frozen for
repeatable visual comparison while rendering animates. Drag to pan and use
the wheel to zoom. The journal supports camp-object inspection.
art-variants.html presents labelled material, plant, object and character states.
animation-study.html shows Tomas's action vocabulary with independent breathing,
blink, gaze, fatigue, shivering, wet clothing, load and sleep examples.
The examples do not change his live state. Live island runs the normal simulation.

SHARED STYLE
The attached forest concept is the common target: fine clustered pixels,
warm ochre earth, olive/sage foliage, copper/gold leaves, blue-teal water,
subdued outlines and upper-left light. The actual island map, object positions,
installed construction geometry and finite inventories are retained.

FAMILIES AND TRANSITIONS
50 PNG files under v2/assets/ground, people, woodland and water.
- Ground: healthy, dry, damp and worn grass/soil copies, blended using finite
  grass, soil moisture and path wear; spatial interpolation avoids tile squares.
- Rocky ground: neutral, damp, mossy and dry textures. ROCK is the flat patch
  in the supplied screenshot. Embedded cobbles are terrain material; boulders,
  gathered stones and flint remain separate simulated objects.
- Trees: summer/autumn copies, 64 colour stages, finer growth increments,
  finite clustered leaf loss and snow. Stable twig positions become visible
  as deciduous leaves fall. Trunks have finer bark grain and birch markings.
- Fern, bramble, gorse: four growth/season/flower/fruit/dormant copies.
- Reeds: short, green, seed-head and autumn copies, blended from plant state.
- Tomas: neutral, down-looking, closed-eye and tired head copies. His original
  jointed skeleton handles action, tool, contact, stance and carrying geometry.
  Independent physiological clocks produce varied combinations over his actions;
  fatigue/pain affect posture and stride, cold drives shivering, clothing wetness
  changes fabric, and even sleeping Tomas breathes. No finite animation bank is
  needed. Held work endpoints and planted feet survive secondary body motion.
- Driftwood: dry, wet, weathered and snow copies. The live scene uses moisture
  and snow; the weathered copy is available when a caller supplies a decay state.
- Rocks: identity shading, gradual wetness and snow presentation.
- Wildlife: fine jointed dog, smoother fine gull/rabbit motion, hatch-cover raft.
- Camp: fine installed-part timber/thatch, grain, binding, construction progress,
  condition, household objects, material piles, snares, food remains and pots.
- Water: authored fine texture with actual depth/wind/ice/rain/foam presentation;
  reflections of nearby trees, reeds and rocks; finite marine driftwood and stream
  leaf debris; exposed mussel/cockle beds; distant vessels. Submerged stones are
  bed texture. The renderer spawns no physical decorative inventory.
